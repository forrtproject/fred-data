FLoRA audit decisions

Decide what should happen to each audited FLoRA record. Decisions are saved in this browser until you export them; nothing here edits FLoRA.

    About this review

    Explore field decisions, supporting evidence, and correction proposals. Each case shows what is wrong with a FLoRA record, the current and proposed values, the evidence, and any dependency that must be settled first.

    Current values come from the frozen FLoRA snapshot that was audited. Recording a decision never changes FLoRA: decisions stay in this browser, and an exported file is a list of decisions for whoever applies the changes. In audit notes, “parent” means the integrating reviewer who applies decisions.

    Shortened evidence excerpts are embedded in this page.
