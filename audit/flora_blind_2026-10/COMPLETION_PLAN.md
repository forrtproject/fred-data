# Remaining evidence work

The original bounded audit attempted all 2,344 works and produced results for all 2,818 input rows. The user authorised a supplemental review of the 102 previously uncalled field requests across 87 works. This queue is running separately from the completed original run; 55 works have abstracts and 32 have full text. All evidence rules remain unchanged.

Before supplemental integration, 836 rows were not fully resolved: 453 with full text, 330 with abstracts, and 53 without usable evidence. Field-specific gaps were 466 types, 463 original identities, 791 replication outcomes, 29 computational reproducibility outcomes, and 19 robustness outcomes. These counts overlap within rows and works. The 102 requests are a subset of this backlog.

Completing the supplemental queue requires collecting all Argon replies, checking literal quotations and their scientific scope, resolving objections within the two-attempt budget, and regenerating and verifying the comparison, report, coverage accounting, and draft PR. Unsupported replies remain unverified; a completed review does not guarantee a resolved field.

Resolving the wider audit backlog would additionally require:

- Recovering adequate primary text and fresh eligible coding for the 55 works without usable coding. The reported skip categories are 43 no-text works, three excluded for blinding compromise, eight for paper-identity conflicts, and one for incomplete source scope.
- Seeking full text for abstract-only works where the abstract cannot establish the original identity, outcome, or target completeness. An abstract cannot establish absence from the unseen paper.
- Resolving the 30 previously reviewed works with remaining transport, quotation, identity, or scientific-scope problems. Most have exhausted the original two-attempt budget. Any new review budget must preserve that history and use stronger evidence or an explicit scope decision.
- Assessing the 609 unverified candidate omitted targets. These are unmatched blind targets, not confirmed omissions. Each needs paper-level identity, evidence that this work actually re-tests it, and deduplication against shipped pairs. The current report confirms 107 omissions and excludes 91 candidates.
- Reviewing the accepted corrections before applying them in a separate dataset change. This audit does not alter output/flora.csv or the pipelines.

Some source papers may never support a definitive classification. An exhaustive search can therefore finish with documented unverified fields; forcing every field into a substantive category would weaken the evidence standard. The next useful completion target is a verified disposition for each outstanding request, with inaccessible or insufficient evidence recorded explicitly.
