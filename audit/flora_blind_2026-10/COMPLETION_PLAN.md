## What a wider completion pass needs

First, inspect the 421 unresolved rows that already have full text. Identify which gaps require paper-level target mapping, additional statistical context, literal quotation recovery, or an explicit codebook scope decision. The 31 works that ended without fully validated review replies include previously exhausted reviews and the one new invalid-quotation case; valid replies can also contain undeterminable fields. Any renewed review budget should preserve attempt history and follow stronger evidence or a clearly stated unresolved question.

Second, recover full text for abstract-only rows where the abstract does not settle the field. Independently, 56 works lack usable coding: 43 no-text cases, three excluded for blinding compromise, eight for identity conflicts, and two for incomplete source scope. Failed or excluded codings need primary-source recovery and eligible fresh coding, without exposing blind coders to comparisons. These work counts and the row counts above overlap and should not be added.

Third, assess the 609 unverified candidate omitted targets. These are unmatched blind targets, not confirmed omissions. Each needs paper-level identity, evidence that this work actually re-tests it, and deduplication against shipped pairs. The current report confirms 107 omissions and excludes 91 candidates.

Finally, review accepted corrections before applying them in a separate dataset change. This audit leaves output/flora.csv and the pipelines unchanged. The original bounded-run history and this supplemental phase remain separate in run_manifest.json.

An exhaustive evidence search can finish with documented unverified fields where the primary source is inaccessible or genuinely insufficient. The practical completion target is a verified disposition for every field and candidate, rather than forcing a substantive category for unsupported claims.
