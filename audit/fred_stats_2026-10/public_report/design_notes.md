# Report design

The report is a technical audit record with an application package. Use a white page (#FCFDFF), dark blue text (#18334B), source-check blue (#176A96), restrained berry for confirmed errors (#9F3654), teal for application guidance (#0D796E), and pale blue-grey (#E7F0F6). Use Segoe UI/Liberation Sans for readable prose and tabular numerals for counts. Keep long paragraphs below 75 characters per line.

Desktop layout: a narrow sticky contents rail beside a continuous report. The opening gives the concrete finding and its denominator; it does not use a generic dashboard card grid. Seven segmented field bars are the one dominant visual, with a fully labelled numeric table immediately below. Corrections have local search, a field filter, and pagination. Downloads and tested R usage are prominent. On mobile, the contents links wrap above the text; wide data tables scroll within their own regions. No animation or externally fetched fonts.

The design was checked against the brief: source availability and field verification have separate headings/denominators, the coverage visual leads to uncertainty rather than a headline error-rate extrapolation, and the patch's 920 cells are distinguished from 493 audited field decisions. Decorative metrics, gradients, and identical rounded cards are omitted.
